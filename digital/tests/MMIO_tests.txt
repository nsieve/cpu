MMIO (MMIO.dig): a 74138 on A0-A2 (A3-A7 aren't decoded, so the registers repeat every
8 bytes), two 74377 to write and two 74245 to read.
  0xBF00  read   keyboard data (KB_IN), and KB_EN goes low while it's read
  0xBF01  read   keyboard status: bit 7 = KB_ST
  0xBF02  write  SPI_OUT (5 bits, D5-D7 aren't used)
  0xBF03  write  DISPLAY
The strobes are active-low (nMEM.dig gates them with the clock). The 74377 clock is
OR(nMMIO_CS, nMEM_WR), so a write is latched when nMEM_WR goes back to 1, and the reads are
OR(nMEM_RD, Y0/Y1 of the 74138). Only one input changes per row around a write: on real
chips, two inputs of that OR changing together could make a glitch on the clock.
