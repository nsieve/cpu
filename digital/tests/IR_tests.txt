Instruction register (IR.dig): two 74377 and two 74245.
  - On the rising clock edge the high byte (the opcode) loads from ROM_IN when nIRH_LD = 0,
    and the low byte when nIRL_LD = 0. A fetch sets both, LDT only nIRL_LD.
  - I15_11, I10_8 and I7_5 are its fields, always on.
  - nIRD_OE puts its low byte on the data bus (I0_7), nIRA_OE on the address bus (AB).
