# Control Bits

Generated by `programs/gen_docs.py`.

The control unit has 3 EPROMs with 16 outputs each, so 48 control bits, but only 43 are used. Bit 0 is output D0 of EPROM 1, bit 16 is D0 of EPROM 2, and bit 32 is D0 of EPROM 3.

Bits that start with `n` are active-low. The programmer writes every bit inactive by default (1 for active-low, 0 for active-high), so an instruction only has to list the bits it uses.

| Bit | Name | EPROM | Pin | Active | Description |
| --- | ---- | ----- | --- | ------ | ----------- |
| 0 | `PC_INC` | 1 | D0 | high | increment the pc |
| 1 | `nPC_LD` | 1 | D1 | low | load the pc (for jmps) |
| 2 | `PC_OE` | 1 | D2 | high | 1 = the pc stops driving the address bus (wired to the active-low OE of its 245s, so by default the pc drives it) |
| 3 | `nPCL_OE` | 1 | D3 | low | pc outputs the lower byte to the data bus |
| 4 | `nPCH_OE` | 1 | D4 | low | pc outputs the higher byte to the data bus |

| 6 | `nIRL_LD` | 1 | D6 | low | load the lower byte of the instruction register from ROM (a fetch sets both) |

| 8 | `nIRA_OE` | 1 | D8 | low | output the lower byte of the ir to the address bus (LB) |
| 9 | `nHBA_OE` | 1 | D9 | low | forces the higher byte of the address bus to 0x00 |
